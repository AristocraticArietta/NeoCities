## About this series
I have found that I go through phases when it comes to my YouTube addiction. Sometimes it's anime AMVs, movie trailers for movies that I probably won't watch, or some random rabbit hole that I go down and then suddenly I know way too much about something. This is a series of those rabbit holes. I hope to keep this series going every month, so I can chronicle my YouTube habits and share some cool stuff with you all. I will update this list throughout the month, so check back and see how it evolves!

## My Favorite Videos - October 2026

Wooo it's been a minute since I've updated this site! I am hoping to get back into the swing of things and update more regularly. Life has been pretty rough lately and I need a distraction lol. My academic life is in shambles but at least I have a good soundtrack. Crash out playlist, anyone?

I've found recently that I've been listening to a lot of music on YouTube, especially the hyperpop and pop girl artists. (When I say girl, I mean femme-presenting people, not just cis women.) Artists like ADÉLA, Charli xcx, LOTS of Slayyyter, Ariana Grande, SOPHIE, JADE, and FLO dominate my feed. I've also been really missing the movies, as I haven't been in a while since moving farther away from my beloved AMC, so expect a lot of movie trailers as well! Anything to boost my confidence during these dark times.
